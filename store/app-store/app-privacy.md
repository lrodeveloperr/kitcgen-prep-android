# App Privacy answers — iOS

## Data collection

**Data Not Collected.** The iOS app has no ads SDK, analytics SDK, account, cloud sync or server endpoint. Boards, templates, task details, settings and timer state are stored locally and are not transmitted to the developer or third parties.

## Tracking

**No.** The app does not track users and does not request App Tracking Transparency permission.
