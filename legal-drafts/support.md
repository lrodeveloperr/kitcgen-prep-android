## Useful information to include

- Android app version;
- device model and Android version;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead; and
- whether the issue involved a timer, ad, privacy form or purchase.

Do not send payment-card information, passwords, authentication secrets or other unnecessary sensitive information.

## Support-message retention

Our normal retention period for support communications is **24 months after the support matter is closed or last substantively active**. We may retain a communication longer where reasonably required for an unresolved dispute, security incident, fraud prevention, tax/accounting requirement, legal claim or another legal obligation.

## Purchases

Android remove-ads subscriptions are billed and managed through Google Play. Use Google Play to manage or cancel an active subscription. Refund eligibility is governed by Google Play policy and applicable mandatory law.

## Privacy

Privacy questions and rights requests may be sent to **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**. Kitchen Prep Board is local-first; ordinary board and task content is not uploaded to GoodUse Studios merely because you use the app.

## Crash and ANR diagnostics

Android 1.0 uses **Google Play Android vitals** for Play-provided crash and ANR reporting. It does not include Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Users may control Android/Google diagnostics sharing through their device/Google settings where available.

## Safety

Kitchen Prep Board is an organizational aid. Do not rely on a timer or task-completion indicator as proof that food is safe or fully cooked. See the separate Safety Notice.
