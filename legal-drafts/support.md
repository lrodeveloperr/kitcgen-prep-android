# Kitchen Prep Board Support — release draft

For help with Kitchen Prep Board, contact **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**.

Please include only the information needed to diagnose the issue. You do not need to send the contents of your kitchen boards for ordinary support. If a screenshot contains workplace or personal information, review it before sending.

## Useful information to include

- Android or iOS;
- app version;
- device model and operating-system version;
- what you expected to happen;
- what happened instead; and
- whether the issue involved a timer, ad, privacy form or purchase.

## Privacy
