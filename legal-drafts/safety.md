# Kitchen Prep Board Safety Notice — release draft

Kitchen Prep Board is an organizational and timing aid. It does **not** determine whether food is safe.

A timer, suggested duration, task status, prep quantity or "Done" state does not prove:

- safe internal temperature or doneness;
- safe cooling, holding, reheating or storage;
- allergen control;
- freedom from cross-contamination;
- compliance with food-service regulations; or
- suitability for a particular person, workplace or jurisdiction.

Always follow applicable health-authority requirements, workplace procedures, recipe or manufacturer instructions and competent professional guidance. Use a calibrated food thermometer and any other required safety controls where appropriate.

Kitchen conditions, equipment, ingredient size, starting temperature, altitude and other factors can change actual cooking and preparation time. Duration and schedule suggestions are therefore estimates. Manual user overrides always take priority over app suggestions.

If an alert is delayed or missed because of device settings, battery restrictions, denied notification permission, exact-alarm access, operating-system behavior or another technical issue, independently verify the food and process rather than relying on the app state.
