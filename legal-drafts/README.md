# Kitchen Prep Board — Store-readiness legal drafts

These files are release drafts for Kitchen Prep Board. They are staged in this private source repository for review and must not be treated as the public effective policies until the placeholders in `STORE-READINESS.md` are resolved and the approved pages are published at stable public HTTPS URLs.

Planned public pages:

- `privacy.md` — Privacy Policy
- `terms.md` — Terms of Use
- `subscriptions.md` — Ads and remove-ads subscription terms
- `support.md` — Support and contact
- `safety.md` — Food-safety and timer disclaimer
- `data-choices.md` — Local data, deletion and privacy choices

Provider draft identity: **Lateef Razaq-Oyetola carrying on business as GoodUse Studios**, Ontario, Canada.

Draft support/privacy contact: **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**.

The Android release described by these drafts is local-first: no account, no developer-operated cloud database, and app-created boards/tasks/templates/timing data remain on the device unless the user deliberately exports or sends information outside the app. The release uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Google UMP for advertising and regional privacy choices, and Google Play Billing for the optional remove-ads subscription.

Before publication, compare these documents against the final signed Android App Bundle and the Play Console Data safety / App content declarations. Any added analytics, crash-reporting, cloud sync, account system, new SDK, new permission, new ad format, or monetization change requires another alignment review.
