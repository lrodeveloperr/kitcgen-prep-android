## 1. Privacy at a glance

Kitchen Prep Board is designed to be local-first. You do not need an account. Boards, prep tasks, quantities, templates, notes, timing information, timer state, app preferences, and locally learned duration information are stored on your device. We do not operate a cloud database that receives this kitchen content.

The free version displays advertising using Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising services may process information from your device to serve, measure and protect ads. Google UMP is used to obtain or manage advertising privacy choices where required. Google Play Billing processes the optional remove-ads subscription on Android.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

Depending on the features you use, Kitchen Prep Board may store locally:

- board names and status;
- prep-task text and ordering;
- pasted or shared task text;
- quantities, units, on-hand values and prep-gap calculations;
- templates, notes and reference URLs you choose to save;
- timing objectives, timer state and task completion state;
- app language, display and notification preferences; and
- locally learned duration observations used to improve future suggestions.

This information is not transmitted to GoodUse Studios merely because you create or use a board. If you deliberately export data, share it, or include it in a support request, the destination you choose may receive it.

## 3. Advertising and Google Mobile Ads

The free version uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. According to Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, the SDK may automatically collect or share information including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
- user interactions with the app or ads;
- diagnostic information; and
- device or other identifiers, such as the advertising identifier or App Set ID where available.

Google may use this information for advertising, measurement/analytics, security and fraud prevention, subject to its terms and applicable law.

Where required by law or Google policy, Google UMP presents a consent or privacy-choice interface before advertising data is used in ways requiring consent. Depending on your region and choices, the app may request personalized, non-personalized or limited ads, or may not request an ad until permitted. You can reopen applicable Google privacy choices from **Settings > Privacy & data > Privacy choices** when Google reports that the form is available.

GoodUse Studios does not sell a database of your kitchen-board content. Advertising-related processing by Google is governed by Google’s own privacy terms and the choices available in the Google consent interface.

## 4. Google Play purchases

On Android, Google Play Billing handles the optional remove-ads subscription. Google processes payment information. Kitchen Prep Board does not receive your full payment-card number.

The app receives information needed to operate the entitlement, such as the product identifier, purchase state, purchase token and whether the purchase is acknowledged. The current Android implementation uses this information on the device to determine whether ads should be shown. Google Play’s terms and privacy policy apply to the payment transaction.

## 5. Support communications

If you contact us by email or another support channel, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, screenshots or diagnostic details you attach. We use it to respond, investigate problems, prevent abuse and meet legal obligations. We do not require you to send the contents of your kitchen boards to obtain ordinary support.

Support communications are retained only as long as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal requirements. The final public policy will state the normal support-retention period once confirmed.

## 6. Permissions and device capabilities

Kitchen Prep Board may use:

- **Notifications** to alert you about timers when you enable or use timer alerts;
- **Exact-alarm access on Android** when needed for reliable time-sensitive timer behavior;
- **Boot-completed and time/time-zone change signals on Android** to recover or recalculate active timer state after device or clock changes; and
- **Internet access** for advertising, Google UMP privacy messaging and Google Play services.

Permission requests should be shown in context when the relevant feature is first needed. Denying an optional permission may reduce timer-alert reliability but should not prevent ordinary local board creation.

## 7. Data retention and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it, clear app storage or uninstall the app, subject to platform behavior. Android backup is disabled in the current release configuration.

**Settings > Privacy & data > Delete local data** deletes the app’s local boards, tasks, templates, timer records and learning history covered by the delete function. Uninstalling the app also removes ordinary app-private local data from the device. Deleting local app data does not cancel a Google Play subscription.

Advertising data and Google Play transaction records are retained by Google under Google’s policies and legal obligations. Where applicable, use the Google privacy-choice interface and Google account/privacy controls for data processed by Google.

## 8. Legal bases and regional rights

Where a law requires a legal basis for personal-data processing, the applicable basis depends on the activity. Examples include consent for advertising processing where consent is required, performance of a requested transaction for subscription functionality, responding to a support request, and compliance with legal obligations. We do not treat acceptance of these Terms as consent to advertising where separate consent is required.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent, objection or restriction, data portability, or to opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale or sharing. Some rights relate to data held by Google rather than by GoodUse Studios because the core kitchen-board data remains only on your device.

To make a privacy request concerning information held by GoodUse Studios, contact **lrodeveloperr@gmail.com**. We may need enough information to verify and respond to the request, but we will not ask for unnecessary kitchen-board content.

## 9. International processing

GoodUse Studios is based in Canada. Google and other platform providers may process advertising, purchase or support-related information in multiple countries. Cross-border processing is subject to the safeguards and terms used by the relevant provider and applicable law. Required EU/UK representative and transfer disclosures will be added before public release if they apply to this deployment.

## 10. Children

**Release assumption requiring confirmation:** Kitchen Prep Board is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. The app should not be configured as child-directed advertising. If the intended audience changes, the advertising configuration, store declarations and this policy must be reassessed before release.

## 11. Security

We use a local-first architecture to reduce unnecessary transmission of kitchen-board data. The Android app disables ordinary application backup, uses app-private storage for its database, and relies on encrypted platform connections for Google services. No security measure can guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, SDKs, advertising practices, legal requirements or business details change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy becomes effective. Material changes will be communicated in a manner appropriate to the change and applicable law.
