# Kitchen Prep Board — Local Data and Privacy Choices

Kitchen Prep Board is designed so ordinary kitchen-board content stays on the device.

## Local data

The app may store boards, tasks, templates, prep-gap quantities, timer state, notes, reference URLs, preferences and learning history in app-private local storage. No account is required.

## Delete local data

Use **Settings > Privacy & data > Delete local data** to delete the app’s local boards, tasks, templates, timer records and learning history covered by the app’s deletion routine.

Deleting local app data does not cancel an Android Google Play subscription. Subscription management is handled through Google Play.

Uninstalling the app also removes ordinary app-private local data, subject to operating-system behavior. Android app backup is disabled in the current release configuration.

## Advertising privacy choices

The free Android version uses Google Mobile Ads / AdMob. Where Google UMP reports that a privacy-options form is required or available, use **Settings > Privacy & data > Privacy choices** to manage applicable advertising choices.

Advertising choices may change whether personalized, non-personalized or limited ads can be requested. They do not change the contents of your locally stored kitchen boards.

## Export and import

The Android backend contains a versioned export/import capability, but the production release must not claim user-accessible export/import until the native document-picker UI is enabled and tested in the signed build.
